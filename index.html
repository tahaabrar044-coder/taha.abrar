01 / About me

Research with a purpose.

Bringing together biological sciences and emerging technologies to explore practical research questions in plant health.

My academic interests sit at the intersection of biotechnology, fungal microbiology and nanobiotechnology. Through my MPhil research, I have explored the green synthesis of copper nanoparticles using plant extract and their antifungal potential against fungal pathogens associated with canker stain of Platanus orientalis.

I am now interested in pursuing doctoral research where I can deepen my understanding of molecular plant–pathogen interactions, fungal biology and innovative disease-management strategies.

02 / Featured research

Where my research takes shape.

A closer look at the MPhil research connecting plant-based synthesis with fungal pathogen research.

MPhil research project

Green synthesis of copper nanoparticles using Eucalyptus extract and evaluation of their antifungal activity against fungal pathogens associated with canker stain of Platanus orientalis.

This project investigates a plant-mediated route for synthesising copper nanoparticles and examines their antifungal activity against relevant fungal isolates. It brings together green nanotechnology, fungal microbiology and plant pathology to explore potential applications in disease management.
